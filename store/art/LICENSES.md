## Hero photo (one image, D7)

Rules: night or dusk runner, seen from the back or side or as a silhouette. No faces, no visible logos or bib numbers, no yellow. Unsplash and Pexels give no model or property releases, which is why faces and logos are excluded. Re-read the licence on the day of download; do not rely on this file's summary.
