Delete your Run companion data

Run Supreme, Android. Last updated 25 October 2026.

The Run companion and its coach are optional. If you turned them on, our server holds your run summaries, your answers to its questions, notes you typed, the notes it sent you, and your Telegram chat (or, later, WhatsApp number). If you chatted with the coach, it also holds your coach chats (kept 30 days). If you let the coach remember you, it holds the coach's memory: short notes about your goals, schedule and preferences, a summary of earlier chats, and, only if you ticked it, your health notes. You can delete all of it. You do not need the app.

Option 1: send DELETE in the chat

  1. Open the Run companion chat in Telegram (or WhatsApp, once available).
  2. Send the word DELETE.
  3. The companion erases everything it holds about you and replies to confirm.

This works even if you have uninstalled the app.

Option 2: in the app

Settings, Companion, Delete everything.

Option 3: ask us by email

If you cannot reach the chat or the app, email privacy@taiaroa.xyz from any address and say you want your Run companion data deleted. Tell us the Telegram username or phone number you used, so we can find it. We will delete it and reply to confirm.

Deleting only the coach

  • Coach memory only: send /memory in Telegram to see every note and delete any of them, or tap Turn off memory in Settings, Companion, which deletes all of it at once.
  • Health notes only: untick "Keep health notes too" in Settings, Companion. The health notes and the chat summary are deleted at once.
  • The coach only: tap Turn off coach chat in Settings, Companion. That deletes your coach chats and all coach memory; your run summaries stay until you delete them as above.

What happens next

  • Your data is removed from the live server at once.
  • Encrypted nightly backups are deleted after 14 days, so it is gone from them within 14 days.
  • Meta may keep WhatsApp delivery records for up to 30 days. Telegram keeps the chat under its own policy.
  • Notes already in your chat stay there until you delete the chat yourself.
  • Coach chat requests go through OpenRouter to Claude models hosted by Google Cloud or Amazon Web Services, and we ask each for zero data retention, so we expect them to hold nothing to delete. The coach's web searches, if any, go to a search provider the same way. We cannot delete anything at those providers ourselves.
  • Our server's per-tester activity counts (kept 120 days) and your coach chats (kept 30 days) are deleted at once with the rest.

If you never turned the companion on, we hold nothing about you. Your runs stay on your phone.

Full details are in the privacy policy, section 2b.
